# Cintia Millan

PhD researcher at NOVA IMS (Lisbon), studying multi-agent systems and evolutionary game theory: when the mechanisms that sustain cooperation (reputation, punishment, network structure) hold up as AI agents shift from human-supervised to autonomous, reward-driven adaptation, and when they quietly break.

At Web Summit, I drive how LLM agents get integrated into our infrastructure, treated as production systems that have to integrate cleanly and fail predictably, not a novelty layer.

## Research

- **[fragility-of-cooperation-hybrid-ai](https://github.com/CiMillan/fragility-of-cooperation-hybrid-ai)**: my PhD research plan (NOVA IMS). When cooperation holds up in hybrid human-AI societies, and when the classical mechanisms that sustain it break under different adaptive rules. Supervised by Flávio L. Pinheiro and Ian J. Scott.
- **[multi-agent-system-RL](https://github.com/CiMillan/multi-agent-system-RL)**: the validated engine behind a diagnostic tool I'm building for cooperation fragility in multi-agent AI systems. Replicates a *Scientific Reports* paper (Tang, Wang & Xing, 2025); validation status and open issues are documented in the repo.
- **[pos-validator-game](https://github.com/CiMillan/pos-validator-game)**: game-theoretic model of Proof-of-Stake validator concentration, calibrated against Ethereum and Cosmos.

## Also building

- **[summit-agent](https://github.com/CiMillan/summit-agent)**: a Ruby framework for tool-using LLM agents with a real local-LLM backend (Ollama), not a mock.
- **[volatility-forecast](https://github.com/CiMillan/volatility-forecast)**: volatility forecasting comparing GARCH, XGBoost, and LSTM with walk-forward validation.
